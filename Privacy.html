Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026 — DaVinci Music Group LLC / davincimusic.io — How emails, cookies, wallets, and IPFS data are used.

Effective Date: November 9, 2026
Company: DaVinci Music Group LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our")
Platform: davincimusic.io

DaVinci Music Group LLC respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit davincimusic.io, utilize our tokenized digital marketplace, upload content to IPFS, or interact with our smart contracts and services.

1. Information We Collect

Public Blockchain & Wallet Data: When you connect a Web3 wallet (e.g., MetaMask, WalletConnect), we collect and store your public wallet address. Blockchain transactions, token minting logs (including DVX token rewards), and smart contract interactions are inherently public.

Account & Subscriber Data: Information you provide when registering, such as your legal name, email address, display name, profile details, and payment/payout information.

Uploaded Media & Metadata: Audio, video, and image files uploaded to create IPFS URIs, along with associated metadata (title, artwork, track metadata, composition credits).

Usage & Analytics Data: IP addresses, browser types, interaction data (such as verified clicks, streams, and views used to calculate DVX minting rewards), device information, and cookies.

Emails & Cookies: davincimusic.io utilizes personal data like emails and cookies — emails for account, royalty, and communication, cookies for analytics, session, and DVX verification.

2. How We Use Your Information

Operate the marketplace, generate IPFS URIs and execute smart contracts, track metrics to mint DVX, calculate royalty payouts (50% master / publishing), prevent fraud / wash trading / bots, comply with legal / tax / FinCEN BOI requirements.

3. IPFS & Blockchain Transparency

Files uploaded to IPFS are pinned and distributed across decentralized nodes, publicly accessible via CID/URI. Data on public blockchains (wallet addresses, token balances, royalty transfers) is immutable and cannot be deleted by DaVinci Music Group LLC.

4. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal contact information. We may share with service providers (hosting, analytics, IPFS), publishing & royalty partners (ASCAP, SESAC, HFA, MLC), and for legal compliance.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures. No system is 100% secure. We retain records as long as necessary for contractual and legal obligations.

6. Your Rights & Contact

You may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of off-chain profile data depending on jurisdiction.
